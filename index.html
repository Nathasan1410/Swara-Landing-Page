Disasters do not only break roads. They break access.

People may still have a phone, but no signal. They may be panicked, exhausted, cold, injured, or responsible for someone else. The knowledge exists, but it is often somewhere unreachable.

What Swara is built for

Swara does not replace responders. It focuses on the gap before better help arrives: basic guidance, local access, and phone-to-phone handoff.

Offline guides first

Emergency categories and survival steps work without internet and without the Gemma model installed.

Ask Swara locally

Capable devices can run Gemma 4 E2B-it through LiteRT-LM for focused, guide-grounded emergency answers.

Pass it forward

Answers, guides, local web access, app packages, and model files can move through QR or local host mode.

Base features

The product has two jobs: give guidance when the internet is gone, and help that guidance move to the next person.

Guide layer

Offline Survival Guides

Category-based instructions for medical emergencies, fire, flood, stranded conditions, and other urgent cases.

AI layer

Ask Swara / AI Mode

Emergency category routing, optional local document retrieval, bundled survival packs, response modes, and safety-framed local inference.

Handoff

QR-Based Sharing

Share answers, guides, modules, or local host links without cloud accounts or mobile data.

Distribution

Local Host Mode

A prepared phone can host the app package, guide pack, local web version, and model file over a hotspot or local network.

Knowledge

Expandable Guide System

Imported documents and guide modules create the foundation for regional, expert-reviewed, or community-reviewed emergency packs.

Access

Read-Aloud Guidance

Answers can be read aloud when users are overwhelmed, helping someone, or unable to focus on the screen.

Practical reach

Why LiteRT-LM matters

The current Gemma 4 E2B-it LiteRT-LM package is around 2GB instead of the roughly 10GB official safetensors model package.

That matters because the model is not only something Swara runs locally. It is also something the app may need to pass from one prepared phone to another when internet access is unavailable.

In 5 GHz hotspot tests, transferring the roughly 2GB model file took 3 to 8 minutes. The writeup treats this conservatively as a 3 to 15 minute transfer window.

Not raw chat. Guide-grounded emergency assistance.

Ask Swara routes the emergency category, searches imported local guide context when available, attaches the relevant survival pack, then runs Gemma 4 E2B-it on-device through LiteRT-LM.
